The trouble began during a 
severe and unforgiving famine. 
While the animals of the earth grew 
lean and hollow-eyed, a grand feast was 
declared in the heavens by the winged folk. 
Tortoise watched the brilliant birds drift 
effortlessly up into the clouds, their songs 
drifting down like teasing echoes of roasted 
yams, palm wine, and honey. Desperate for a 
taste of the divine banquet, Tortoise hatched a 
classic scheme. He went among the birds with a 
honeyed tongue and a false heart, begging each
 one to gift him a single feather so that he, 
 too, might fly to the sky. Moved by his feigned
  humility, the generous birds plucked out feathers
   and fashioned him a magnificent pair of wings.
